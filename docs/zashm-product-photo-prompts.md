# ZASHM — six standalone product photo prompts

Six **standalone** prompts. Each one is complete on its own: copy it as-is,
replace only `{PRODUCT}`, `{COLOUR}` and `{FABRIC}`, and attach the reference
images. No extra "base" or "negative" block is needed — every prompt already
contains them.

The **ZASHM Signature Backdrop** and the **camera / output block** are written
word-for-word identically in all six, so the whole gallery stays consistent.

- `{PRODUCT}` — e.g. `textured zip jacket`, `tailored trousers`, `linen shirt`
- `{COLOUR}` — e.g. `deep wine burgundy`
- `{FABRIC}` — e.g. `textured woven fabric`
- For prompt 5 only: attach the model photo as the first reference image and
  replace `{MODEL PHOTO}`. The garment reference images stay attached too.

Master output: **1500 × 2000 px, vertical 3:4**. Card derivative: **750 × 1000px**.

---

## 1. FRONT

> Photorealistic luxury e-commerce product photography for ZASHM. Subject: a
> {PRODUCT} in {COLOUR}, made of {FABRIC}. Flat-lay front view, fully fastened,
> symmetrical and centred: collar or neckline open and even, sleeves relaxed
> along the body with no awkward folds, hem perfectly straight, closure, stitching
> and weave clearly legible. Garment occupies about 90% of the frame width with a
> thin, even halo of background around it — no empty side bands, no large empty
> band at the top.
>
> ZASHM Signature Backdrop (identical for the whole gallery): seamless
> warm-charcoal studio gradient from #211E1C at the centre to #2B2724 at the
> edges, matte and non-reflective, reaching all four edges of the frame. No props,
> no floor line, no horizon, no text, no logo, no watermark, no corner hotspot,
> no vignette, no colour cast. A subtle warm champagne rim light separates the
> garment from the background. Soft diffused key light from camera-left at 45°,
> gentle fill from the right, neutral 5600K white balance.
>
> Camera and finish: Phase One XF IQ4, 85mm lens, f/8, ISO 100, RAW capture,
> straight-on and centred, no lens distortion, no perspective warp. Physically
> accurate fabric drape and soft natural contact shadows, razor-sharp textile
> texture, true-to-life colour, 100% photorealistic — real garment, not CGI, not
> illustration, not a 3D render, no plastic or waxy surface.
>
> Output: 1500 × 2000 px, vertical 3:4 portrait, full-bleed crop. Export a
> 750 × 1000 px card derivative. Negative: illustration, painting, CGI, 3D
> render, cartoon, deformed garment, extra sleeves, duplicated collar, mismatched
> panels, unrealistic fabric, bright red backdrop, harsh hotspot, heavy vignette,
> empty margins, floating text, logos, watermarks, oversaturated colour.
