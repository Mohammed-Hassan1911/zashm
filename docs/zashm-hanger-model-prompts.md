# ZASHM — hanger front / hanger back / model prompts

Four **standalone** prompts for the lean gallery: front on a hanger, back on a
hanger, an on-model shot at global-brand editorial standard, and an inside-fabric
macro.

Replace `{PRODUCT}`, `{COLOUR}`, `{FABRIC}`. For prompt 3 attach `{MODEL PHOTO}`
as the first reference image. Everything else — backdrop, lighting, camera and
output — is written word-for-word identically in all three so the gallery stays
consistent. Nothing here was generated, uploaded or deployed.

## 1. FRONT — on hanger

> Photorealistic luxury e-commerce product photography for ZASHM. Subject: a
> {PRODUCT} in {COLOUR}, made of {FABRIC}, hanging on a clothes hanger. For tops
> and dresses use a matte dark-walnut shoulder hanger with a slim
> brushed-champagne metal hook; for trousers and skirts use a clip hanger in the
> same finish. The garment is empty — no mannequin, no body inside — hanging
> naturally with the correct shoulder shape, sleeves or straps falling straight,
> a level hem and the closure fastened; no clips marking or denting the fabric.
> Straight-on front view, perfectly centred. The hook is fully visible with only
> minimal headroom; the garment fills about 90% of the frame width with a thin,
> even halo of background — no empty side bands and no large empty band at the
> top. The hanger reads premium but quiet, never a distraction from the garment.
>
> ZASHM Signature Backdrop (identical across the whole gallery): seamless
> warm-charcoal studio infinity wall, #211E1C at the centre easing to #2B2724 at
> the edges, matte and non-reflective, reaching all four edges of the frame. No
> props, no floor line, no horizon, no text, no logo, no watermark, no corner
> hotspot, no vignette, no colour cast. Soft diffused key light from camera-left
> at 45°, gentle fill from the right, a subtle warm champagne rim light that
> separates the garment from the dark backdrop, neutral 5600K white balance.
>
> Camera and finish: Phase One XF IQ4, 85mm lens, f/8, ISO 100, RAW capture,
> straight-on and centred, level horizon, no lens distortion, no perspective
> warp. Tack-sharp stitching and textile texture, physically accurate drape and a
> soft natural shadow behind the hanging garment, true-to-life colour, 100%
> photorealistic — a real photograph of a real garment, not CGI, not illustration,
> not a 3D render, no plastic or waxy surface.
>
> Output: 1500 × 2000 px, vertical 3:4 portrait, full-bleed. Export a
> 750 × 1000 px card derivative. Negative: illustration, painting, CGI, 3D
> render, cartoon, mannequin, deformed garment, extra sleeves, duplicated collar
> or waistband, wrong colour, bright red backdrop, harsh corner hotspot, heavy
> vignette, empty side margins, floating text, logos, watermarks, oversaturated
> colour.

## Gallery order recommendation

1. **Front on hanger** — first image is the card thumbnail and LCP; it must
   identify the product instantly.
2. **Model** — shows fit and scale.
3. **Back on hanger** — completes the information.

Repeat this order per colour, and keep the first image of the default colour as
the gallery hero.
